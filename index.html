About

I'm Mati Rojas, a Chilean filmmaker, editor and creative director with more than 7 years behind the camera. I film mostly outdoor sports, because I live them too: snow, surf, bike and the mountains are where I feel at home.

I work as a one-man crew with a high-production mindset. I shoot, direct, edit and grade everything myself, so every frame is thought out from the first run of the day to the final master. Snowboarding is my specialty: I ride, so I film follow cam right behind the rider, through every turn, drop and landing.

I've filmed for DC Shoes, Burton and the SLASH Experience with Gigi Rüf, and made campaigns and content for fashion brands like Falabella and Americanino.
